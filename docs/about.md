# About Me

Hi, I'm **DobeShadow** — a Materials &amp; Chemical Engineering student preparing for graduate school, with a side hustle as a self-taught programmer who builds Minecraft plugins.

## Research Direction

I'm about to head into grad school for **electrocatalysis** — using electricity to drive chemical reactions, and steering it toward sustainable biomass conversion. My reading list right now is all about OER, selective oxidation and in-situ characterization, and I'm slowly building the skills to do mechanistic work (DFT, operando spectroscopies) instead of just stacking performance numbers.
